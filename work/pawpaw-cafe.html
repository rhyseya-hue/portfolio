01 / The approach

From curiosity to a table.

Pawpaw is several experiences in one space: all-day brunch, Thai fusion dinners, and functions. The website needs to help a first-time guest understand the difference without making a regular hunt for the menu or booking link.

I developed a responsive front end that puts the guest journey first. Food photography and clear menu sections introduce the café, while opening-hours information and booking actions stay close to the decision to visit.
